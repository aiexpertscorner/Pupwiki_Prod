# How to Handle Submissive Urination in Pups

Finding a puddle on the floor when greeting your puppy can be disheartening.  Submissive or excitement urination is a common but often misunderstood puppy behaviour.  Young dogs may squat and urinate during greetings as a way to communicate appeasement or because they lack full bladder control【294026789387178†L43-L65】.  This article explains why it happens and how to manage it without damaging your puppy’s confidence.

## Excitement Versus Submissive Urination

### Excitement urination
Puppies have immature bladder muscles.  When they get excited—during play or greeting family members—small amounts of urine may leak involuntarily【294026789387178†L43-L65】.  This usually resolves as the puppy matures, but supportive management helps.

### Submissive urination
Some dogs pee during greetings to communicate appeasement; they may hunch, tuck their tail, lower their head, yawn or even roll over and pee【294026789387178†L96-L114】.  It’s a way of saying “I mean no harm.”

## How to Manage and Reduce Accidents

1. **Stay calm during greetings.**  Keep arrivals and departures low‑key.  Avoid loud voices, quick movements or leaning over the puppy, which can trigger urination【294026789387178†L73-L90】.

2. **Take greetings outside.**  Meet your puppy outdoors or on easy‑to‑clean surfaces and allow them to empty their bladder before saying hello【294026789387178†L73-L90】.

3. **Teach impulse control.**  Practise rewarding your puppy for sitting or lying on a mat before receiving attention.  This builds self‑control and reduces excitement【294026789387178†L73-L90】.

4. **Boost confidence.**  Provide positive socialisation experiences with a variety of people, animals and environments.  Agility, nose work and other activities can build self‑assurance【294026789387178†L125-L134】.

5. **Ignore accidents.**  Scolding will only increase anxiety and may worsen submissive urination.  Clean accidents calmly and focus on reinforcing appropriate toileting behaviour.

6. **Visit the vet if needed.**  Urinary tract infections, spay incontinence or other medical problems can cause accidents; consult your veterinarian if accidents persist beyond puppyhood【294026789387178†L43-L65】.

## Summary

Excitement and submissive urination are phases many puppies outgrow.  Stay calm, provide appropriate outlets and build your puppy’s confidence through positive socialisation and training.  With patience and consistency, accidents will become a distant memory.
