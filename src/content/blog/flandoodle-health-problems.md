## About Flandoodles
Flandoodle is a designer cross between the Bouvier des Flandres and the Poodle (Standard). This mix usually weighs about 66-99 pounds, often has a curly, low-shed-leaning coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Glaucoma, Heart issues.

### What to know about Flandoodle health planning
Flandoodles have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Glaucoma, Heart issues**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Flandoodle are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Flandoodle life expectancy of 10–15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Flandoodle health
**What are the most common health problems in Flandoodles?**
Flandoodles have 2 documented genetic health conditions: Glaucoma, Heart issues. These are breed-level statistical risks — not every Flandoodle will develop these conditions. Your vet can recommend targeted screening based on your dog's individual history.
**How long do Flandoodles live?**
Flandoodles have a typical life expectancy of 10–15 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Flandoodle?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Flandoodles. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.
### Care tips for Flandoodle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
