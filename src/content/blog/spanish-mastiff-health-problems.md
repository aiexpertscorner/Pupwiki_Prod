## About Spanish Mastiffs
The Spanish Mastiff is a dog of great size, well-balanced, very powerful and muscular. He has a massive head and a body covered with a medium-length coat. His bark is raucous, low pitched and deep, very sonorous and audible from a considerable distance. ### What to know about Spanish Mastiff health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Spanish Mastiff in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Spanish Mastiff are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Spanish Mastiff life expectancy of 10–12 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
