## About Irish Wolfhounds
Irish Wolfhounds are large-sized (105–120 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a rough coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### What to know about Irish Wolfhound health planning
Irish Wolfhounds have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **heart, liver, hips**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Irish Wolfhound are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Irish Wolfhound life expectancy of 6–8 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
