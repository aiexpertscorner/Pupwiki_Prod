## About Bedlington Terriers
Bedlington Terriers are small-sized (17–23 lbs), with active energy, low shedding, and a curly coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Bedlington Terriers
Bedlington Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: liver, eye problems. Typical life expectancy is 11–16 years.
- **Liver**: Ask your vet about recommended screening and prevention for this condition in Bedlington Terriers.

### What to know about Bedlington Terrier health planning
Bedlington Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **liver, eye problems**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Bedlington Terrier often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Bedlington Terrier life expectancy of 11–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Bedlington Terrier health
**What are the most common health problems in Bedlington Terriers?**
Bedlington Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: liver, eye problems. These are breed-level statistical risks — not every Bedlington Terrier will develop these conditions. Your vet can recommend targeted screening based on your dog's individual history.
**How long do Bedlington Terriers live?**
Bedlington Terriers have a typical life expectancy of 11–16 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. **Should I get pet insurance for a Bedlington Terrier?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Bedlington Terriers. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.

- Needs 60-90 minutes active exercise daily; thrives with a job or sport.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.
