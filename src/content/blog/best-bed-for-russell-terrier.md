## How to choose for a Russell Terrier
Measure your dog stretched out fully, add 12 inches, then match to the bed's stated usable sleep surface. Check whether the cover is machine washable.
### What to look for in a bed for a Russell Terrier
Measure your Russell Terrier when fully stretched — add 12 inches to that length for the ideal sleep surface. Smaller breeds often prefer bolster or donut-style beds that let them curl up, rather than flat mats. Orthopedic foam matters most once your Russell Terrier reaches 7+ years — earlier than most owners expect for medium breeds. If your dog is already showing joint stiffness, prioritise pressure-relief foam over aesthetics. Active Russell Terriers tend to dig and circle before lying down — look for beds with reinforced stitching at the corners and a water-resistant inner liner to extend the usable lifespan.
### Frequently asked questions about Russell Terrier beds
**What size bed does a Russell Terrier need?**
Measure your Russell Terrier when fully stretched out and add 12 inches. Small breeds often sleep curled up but still need space to stretch — a 24–30 inch bed usually covers most small-breed dogs.
**Is an orthopedic bed worth it for a Russell Terrier?**
For any dog over 7 years, or one already showing joint stiffness, an orthopedic foam bed offers meaningful pressure relief. For younger healthy Russell Terriers, a quality foam bed with a washable cover is adequate — you can upgrade as they age.
**How often should I replace my Russell Terrier's bed?**
Most dog beds need replacing every 1–2 years under regular use. Signs it's time: visible compression of the foam (it no longer springs back), persistent odour after washing, or your Russell Terrier choosing the floor over the bed.
