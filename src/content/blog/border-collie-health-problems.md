## About Border Collies
Border Collies are medium-sized (30–55 lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Border Collies
Border Collies have 2 documented genetic health conditions: eye problems, deafness. Typical life expectancy is 12–15 years.
- **Eye problems**: Ask your vet about recommended screening and prevention for this condition in Border Collies.

### Frequently asked questions about Border Collie health
**What are the most common health problems in Border Collies?**
Border Collies have 2 documented genetic health conditions: eye problems, deafness. These are breed-level statistical risks — not every Border Collie will develop these conditions. Your vet can recommend targeted screening based on your dog's individual history.
**How long do Border Collies live?**
Border Collies have a typical life expectancy of 12–15 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Border Collie?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Border Collies. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.
### Care tips for Border Collie owners
- Requires 90+ minutes vigorous exercise daily — fetch, agility, or long hikes.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.
- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
