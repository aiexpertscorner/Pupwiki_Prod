## How to choose for a Staffordshire Bull Terrier
Prioritise formulas with an AAFCO adequacy statement — this confirms the recipe is complete and balanced. Named proteins should appear before grains in the ingredient list. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Staffordshire Bull Terrier is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. Staffordshire Bull Terriers are documented for joint conditions (hip problems) — look for formulas listing glucosamine and chondroitin as added ingredients, or plan to add a separate joint supplement after age 5. For a typical adult Staffordshire Bull Terrier (24–38 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 508 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 732–894 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.

### Frequently asked questions about Staffordshire Bull Terrier feeding
**How much should I feed my Staffordshire Bull Terrier?**
A rough starting point for Staffordshire Bull Terriers (24–38 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Staffordshire Bull Terrier?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Staffordshire Bull Terrier from puppy to adult food?**
Most Staffordshire Bull Terriers can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
