## About Whoodles
Whoodle is a designer cross between the Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier and the Poodle (Standard). This mix usually weighs about 33-44 pounds, often has a curly, low-shed-leaning coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Protein-losing nephropathy (Kidney), PRA.

### Care tips for Whoodle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Consistency matters—keep sessions short, reward-based, and started early for best results.
