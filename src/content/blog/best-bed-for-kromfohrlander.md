## How to choose for a Kromfohrlander
Measure your dog stretched out fully, add 12 inches, then match to the bed's stated usable sleep surface. Check whether the cover is machine washable.
### What to look for in a bed for a Kromfohrlander
Mid-size breeds like the Kromfohrlander do well in rectangular flat beds (30–36 inch) with a removable washable cover. Washability matters more than most buyers expect — plan to launder every 2–3 weeks. Orthopedic foam matters most once your Kromfohrlander reaches 7+ years — earlier than most owners expect for medium breeds. If your dog is already showing joint stiffness, prioritise pressure-relief foam over aesthetics. Choose a cover with a zipper rather than a slip-on sleeve so the insert stays in place even when your Kromfohrlander rearranges it overnight.
### Frequently asked questions about Kromfohrlander beds
**What size bed does a Kromfohrlander need?**
Measure your Kromfohrlander stretched out and add 12 inches. Medium breeds (30–40 lb) typically need a 30–36 inch bed. If they hang off the edges regularly, size up.
**Is an orthopedic bed worth it for a Kromfohrlander?**
For any dog over 7 years, or one already showing joint stiffness, an orthopedic foam bed offers meaningful pressure relief. For younger healthy Kromfohrlanders, a quality foam bed with a washable cover is adequate — you can upgrade as they age.
**How often should I replace my Kromfohrlander's bed?**
Most dog beds need replacing every 1–2 years under regular use. Signs it's time: visible compression of the foam (it no longer springs back), persistent odour after washing, or your Kromfohrlander choosing the floor over the bed.
