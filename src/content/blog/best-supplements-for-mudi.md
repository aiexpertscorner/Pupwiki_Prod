## About Mudis
The Mudi (pronounced “moodie”) is a medium-sized herding dog from Hungary which has been in existence since the 19th century. It is said the Mudi evolved naturally from crosses of the Puli, Pumi and German Spitz breeds. Today, the Mudi, though very rare, is seen as an active, intelligent, biddable working breed. It is estimated there are no more than a few thousand Mudi worldwide, with the greatest numbers being in Hungary, followed by Finland, and then even scarcer throughout Europe, the U.S, and Canada. The Mudi excels at agility, obedience, and flyball, as well as other dog sports. ## How to approach supplements for Mudis
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to look for in supplements for a Mudi
⚠️ Always discuss supplements with your vet before starting. Supplements are not regulated as rigorously as medications — quality and dosing vary widely between products. No breed-specific supplement priority is indicated by current genetic data for the Mudi. A balanced AAFCO-compliant diet is the baseline — supplements fill specific gaps identified by your vet, not general prevention. When evaluating supplements, prioritise products with NASC (National Animal Supplement Council) certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for third-party testing, and a clearly stated active-ingredient concentration on the label.
### Frequently asked questions about Mudi supplements
**What supplements are most relevant for a Mudi?**
No breed-specific supplement priority is clearly indicated for the Mudi. Omega-3 fatty acids are broadly supported for coat health and inflammation. Joint supplements become relevant around age 5+ for many breeds. Always confirm with your vet before starting any supplement.
**Are supplements safe for Mudis?**
Dog supplements are not FDA-regulated the same way medications are — quality varies significantly between brands. Choose products with NASC certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party testing, and clearly stated active ingredient concentrations. Always disclose supplement use to your vet, as interactions with medications and health conditions are possible.
**At what age should I start supplements for a Mudi?**
Most supplements are most relevant from middle age onward (5–7 years depending on size). Puppies should generally not receive adult-strength supplements — ask your vet about puppy-appropriate options if indicated.
### Care tips for Mudi owners

- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
