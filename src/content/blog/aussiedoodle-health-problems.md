## About Aussiedoodles
Aussiedoodle is a designer cross between the Australian Shepherd and the Poodle (Standard). This mix usually weighs about 24-71 pounds, often has a curly, low-shed-leaning coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include MDR1 Gene Mutation, Cataracts, Hip dysplasia. Originally from United States.

### Care tips for Aussiedoodle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Keep body weight lean and monitor mobility to reduce stress on developing joints.
