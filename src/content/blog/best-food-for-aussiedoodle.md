## Why this guide exists for Aussiedoodles

This page helps Aussiedoodle people compare useful brands, products and services for a real care decision. It is also useful if you are still deciding whether a Aussiedoodle fits your home, budget and routine.

Look for an AAFCO nutritional adequacy statement, a named protein in the first three ingredients, and a formula matched to your dog's life stage and size.
