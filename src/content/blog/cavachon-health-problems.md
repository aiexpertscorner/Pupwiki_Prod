## About Cavachons
Cavachon is a designer cross between the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Bichon Frise. This mix usually weighs about 13-22 pounds, often has a softer silky coat, and often blends a calmer companion temperament with moderate activity needs from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Heart murmurs, Atopy (Skin allergies). Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Cavachon health planning
Cavachons have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Heart murmurs, Atopy (Skin allergies)**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Cavachon often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Cavachon life expectancy of 13–15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Cavachon owners
- Does best with shorter daily walks, enrichment toys, and careful weight management.
- Keep coat maintenance steady with weekly brushing and routine skin, ear, and nail checks.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
