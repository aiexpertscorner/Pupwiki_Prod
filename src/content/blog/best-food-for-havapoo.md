## Why this guide exists for Havapoos
Havapoos are medium-sized (varies lbs), with regular energy, minimal shedding, and a wavy coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
This page helps Havapoo people compare useful brands, products and services for a real care decision. It is also useful if you are still deciding whether a Havapoo fits your home, budget and routine.
