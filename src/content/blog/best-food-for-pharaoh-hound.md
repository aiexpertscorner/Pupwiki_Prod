## How to choose for a Pharaoh Hound
Look for AAFCO compliance — the label should confirm the formula is complete and balanced for your dog's life stage. A named protein (chicken, salmon, beef) should be the first ingredient listed. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Pharaoh Hound is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain.
### What to look for in food for a Pharaoh Hound
Medium breeds like the Pharaoh Hound do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Pharaoh Hounds are active dogs — a higher-protein formula (26–30% crude protein) supports muscle maintenance and sustained energy through their exercise demands.
### Frequently asked questions about Pharaoh Hound feeding
**How much should I feed my Pharaoh Hound?**
Follow the feeding guide on your chosen formula and adjust based on body condition — you should be able to feel (but not see) the ribs. Most Pharaoh Hounds need 2–3 meals per day as adults.
**What food formula works best for a Pharaoh Hound?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Pharaoh Hound from puppy to adult food?**
Most Pharaoh Hounds can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
