## About Italian Greyhounds
Italian Greyhounds are small-sized (7–14 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a short coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Italian Greyhounds
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Italian Greyhound (typical life expectancy: 14–15 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.

### What to know about Italian Greyhound health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Italian Greyhound in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Italian Greyhound often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Italian Greyhound life expectancy of 14–15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Italian Greyhound health
**What are the most common health problems in Italian Greyhounds?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Italian Greyhound. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Italian Greyhounds live?**
Italian Greyhounds have a typical life expectancy of 14–15 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Italian Greyhound?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Italian Greyhound owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Reserved with strangers — early, thorough socialization is critical for this breed.
