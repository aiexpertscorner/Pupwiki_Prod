### What to know about Manchester Terrier (Toy) health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Manchester Terrier (Toy) in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Manchester Terrier (Toy) often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Manchester Terrier (Toy) life expectancy of 15–17 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
