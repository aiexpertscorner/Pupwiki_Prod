## About Poogles
Poogle is a designer cross between the Poodle (Miniature) and the Beagle. This mix usually weighs about 18-31 pounds, often has a curly, low-shed-leaning coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Ear infections, Obesity. Originally from United Kingdom.

## How to approach supplements for Poogles
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to look for in supplements for a Poogle
⚠️ Always discuss supplements with your vet before starting. Supplements are not regulated as rigorously as medications — quality and dosing vary widely between products. Poogles have 2 documented genetic conditions. Supplements relevant to these conditions may offer support — confirm relevance and safety with your vet before starting any. When evaluating supplements, prioritise products with NASC (National Animal Supplement Council) certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for third-party testing, and a clearly stated active-ingredient concentration on the label.
### Frequently asked questions about Poogle supplements
**What supplements are most relevant for a Poogle?**
No breed-specific supplement priority is clearly indicated for the Poogle. Omega-3 fatty acids are broadly supported for coat health and inflammation. Joint supplements become relevant around age 5+ for many breeds. Always confirm with your vet before starting any supplement.
**Are supplements safe for Poogles?**
Dog supplements are not FDA-regulated the same way medications are — quality varies significantly between brands. Choose products with NASC certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party testing, and clearly stated active ingredient concentrations. Always disclose supplement use to your vet, as interactions with medications and health conditions are possible.
**At what age should I start supplements for a Poogle?**
Most supplements are most relevant from middle age onward (5–7 years depending on size). Puppies should generally not receive adult-strength supplements — ask your vet about puppy-appropriate options if indicated.
### Care tips for Poogle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
