## How to approach a German Shepherd Dog puppy's first year
Choose puppy-specific formulas, sizes and training tools. Avoid adult-strength supplements and training methods designed for mature dogs.
### What to prioritise in the first weeks with a German Shepherd Dog puppy
Large-breed puppies like the German Shepherd Dog need large-breed-specific puppy formulas that control calcium and phosphorus ratios. Avoid generic puppy food — rapid growth from inappropriate nutrition is a major cause of joint problems in large breeds. Limit stairs and jumping until growth plates close at 12–18 months. Schedule a vet visit within the first 72 hours of bringing your German Shepherd Dog puppy home. The first-year vet schedule typically includes: 8-week vaccines, 12-week booster, 16-week final puppy booster, 6-month neuter/spay consultation, and 12-month first adult check. The critical socialisation window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds, surfaces, people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A puppy class started at 8–10 weeks provides peer exposure that home training alone cannot replicate.
### Frequently asked questions about German Shepherd Dog puppy care
**What does a German Shepherd Dog puppy need in the first week?**
In the first week: vet check within 72 hours, a large-breed puppy formula (2–3 meals/day — avoid one large meal), a crate sized for an adult German Shepherd Dog, and crate training started with short positive sessions. Limit stairs and jumping — growth plates are open until 12–18 months.
**How do I socialise a German Shepherd Dog puppy?**
The critical window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds (traffic, appliances, children), new surfaces (gravel, carpet, grass, pavement), new people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A structured puppy class started at 8–10 weeks is the most efficient route — it covers socialisation and basic obedience in one place. After 16 weeks the window closes — missed socialisation is harder (not impossible) to compensate for later.
**How much does a German Shepherd Dog puppy cost in the first year?**

Total first-year cost is often 50–100% higher than subsequent years.
### Care tips for German Shepherd Dog owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Heavy shedder — brush 3-4× per week and deShed weekly to manage coat.
- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
