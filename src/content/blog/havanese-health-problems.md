## About Havaneses
Havaneses are small-sized (7–13 lbs), with regular energy, low shedding, and a long coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Havaneses
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Havanese (typical life expectancy: 14–16 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.

### What to know about Havanese health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Havanese in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Havanese often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Havanese life expectancy of 14–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Havanese health
**What are the most common health problems in Havaneses?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Havanese. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Havaneses live?**
Havaneses have a typical life expectancy of 14–16 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Havanese?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Havanese owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.
- Very sociable and people-oriented — early socialization keeps friendliness well-directed.
