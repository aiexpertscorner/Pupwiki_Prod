### Care tips for Afghan Hound owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes active exercise daily; thrives with a job or sport.
- Low shedding — regular brushing prevents tangles; minimal shedding management.
- Coat requires daily brushing to prevent matting; professional grooming every 6-8 weeks.
- Reserved with strangers — early, thorough socialization is critical for this breed.
