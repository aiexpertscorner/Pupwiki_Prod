## How to choose for a Working Kelpie
Look for an AAFCO nutritional adequacy statement and a named protein (chicken, salmon, beef) as the first ingredient. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Working Kelpie is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient.
