## Frequently Asked Questions
### What toys are best for Basset Hounds?
Basset Hounds enjoy moderate play with durable chew toys and interactive puzzles. The most important factor is matching toy durability to your dog's chewing intensity — standard rubber toys are usually sufficient.
### How often should I give my Basset Hound new toys?
Rotate toys every 3–5 days to maintain novelty and engagement. Basset Hounds benefit from the mental stimulation of encountering "new" toys regularly. Keep 4–6 toys active and rotate them.
### Are squeaky toys safe for Basset Hounds?
Squeaky toys are generally safe under supervision, but not for unsupervised chewing — the squeaker is a swallowing hazard once the toy is open. Monitor your dog during play and remove damaged toys immediately.
