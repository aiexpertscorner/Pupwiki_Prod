## How to choose for a Chinese Shar-Pei
Prioritise formulas with an AAFCO adequacy statement — this confirms the recipe is complete and balanced. Named proteins should appear before grains in the ingredient list. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient. For a typical adult Chinese Shar-Pei (45–60 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 760 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 958–1170 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Chinese Shar-Pei
Medium breeds like the Chinese Shar-Pei do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Chinese Shar-Pei needs fewer calories than an athletic one. Chinese Shar-Peis shed seasonally — look for formulas that include omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA from salmon or fish oil) to support coat health from the inside out.
### Frequently asked questions about Chinese Shar-Pei feeding
**How much should I feed my Chinese Shar-Pei?**
A rough starting point for Chinese Shar-Peis (45–60 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Chinese Shar-Pei?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Chinese Shar-Pei from puppy to adult food?**
Most Chinese Shar-Peis can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
