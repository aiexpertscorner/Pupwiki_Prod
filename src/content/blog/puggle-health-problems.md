## About Puggles
Puggle is a designer cross between the Pug and the Beagle. This mix usually weighs about 18-31 pounds, often carries an easy-care short coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Brachycephalic syndrome, Epilepsy, Hip dysplasia. Originally from China / United Kingdom.

### Care tips for Puggle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Expect regular shedding—brush 3-4 times per week to control loose coat and skin buildup.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Watch heat and exercise tolerance closely, especially in warm weather or high humidity.
