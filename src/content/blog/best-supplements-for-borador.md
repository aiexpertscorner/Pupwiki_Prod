## About Boradors
Borador is a designer cross between the Border Collie and the Labrador Retriever. This mix usually weighs about 40-62 pounds, can inherit a dense double coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Collie Eye Anomaly (CEA), Hip dysplasia. Originally from United Kingdom.

## How to approach supplements for Boradors
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to look for in supplements for a Borador
⚠️ Always discuss supplements with your vet before starting. Supplements are not regulated as rigorously as medications — quality and dosing vary widely between products. Boradors have documented joint conditions (collie eye anomaly (cea), hip dysplasia). Glucosamine and chondroitin are among the most studied supplements for joint support in dogs — discuss appropriate timing with your vet before symptoms appear. When evaluating supplements, prioritise products with NASC (National Animal Supplement Council) certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for third-party testing, and a clearly stated active-ingredient concentration on the label.
### Frequently asked questions about Borador supplements
**What supplements are most relevant for a Borador?**
Boradors have documented joint conditions — glucosamine (500–1,000 mg/day depending on weight), chondroitin (400–800 mg/day), and omega-3 fatty acids are among the most studied options for joint support in dogs. Confirm dosing with your vet.
**Are supplements safe for Boradors?**
Dog supplements are not FDA-regulated the same way medications are — quality varies significantly between brands. Choose products with NASC certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party testing, and clearly stated active ingredient concentrations. Always disclose supplement use to your vet, as interactions with medications and health conditions are possible.
**At what age should I start supplements for a Borador?**
Most supplements are most relevant from middle age onward (5–7 years depending on size). Puppies should generally not receive adult-strength supplements — ask your vet about puppy-appropriate options if indicated.
### Care tips for Borador owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Expect regular shedding—brush 3-4 times per week to control loose coat and skin buildup.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Keep body weight lean and monitor mobility to reduce stress on developing joints.
