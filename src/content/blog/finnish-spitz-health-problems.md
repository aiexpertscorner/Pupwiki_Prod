## About Finnish Spitzs
Finnish Spitzs are medium-sized (20–33 lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Finnish Spitzs
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Finnish Spitz (typical life expectancy: 13–15 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.

### Frequently asked questions about Finnish Spitz health
**What are the most common health problems in Finnish Spitzs?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Finnish Spitz. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Finnish Spitzs live?**
Finnish Spitzs have a typical life expectancy of 13–15 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Finnish Spitz?**

### Care tips for Finnish Spitz owners
- Requires 90+ minutes vigorous exercise daily — fetch, agility, or long hikes.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
