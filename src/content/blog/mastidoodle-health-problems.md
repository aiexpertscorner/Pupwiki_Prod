## About Mastidoodles
Mastidoodle is a designer cross between the Mastiff and the Poodle (Standard). This mix usually weighs about 99-143 pounds, often has a curly, low-shed-leaning coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Joint issues, Bloat.

### What to know about Mastidoodle health planning
Mastidoodles have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Joint issues, Bloat**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Mastidoodle are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Mastidoodle life expectancy of 8–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Mastidoodle owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes of daily walks, play, and light training games.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Keep body weight lean and monitor mobility to reduce stress on developing joints.
