## How to approach Belgian Tervuren care
Look for AAFCO compliance — the label should confirm the formula is complete and balanced for your dog's life stage. A named protein (chicken, salmon, beef) should be the first ingredient listed. Large breeds benefit from formulas with controlled calcium-to-phosphorus ratios and moderate fat content to support joint health and avoid rapid growth issues. Belgian Tervuren is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.

### Frequently asked questions about Belgian Tervuren feeding
**How much should I feed my Belgian Tervuren?**
A rough starting point for Belgian Tervurens (45–75 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Belgian Tervuren?**
Look for formulas specifically labelled for large breeds, which manage calcium and phosphorus ratios to support joint development. Avoid puppy formulas designed for small dogs even if your Belgian Tervuren is young.
**When should I switch my Belgian Tervuren from puppy to adult food?**
Large breeds should stay on a large-breed puppy formula until 18–24 months, then transition to an adult formula. Switching too early can disrupt joint development during the growth phase.
