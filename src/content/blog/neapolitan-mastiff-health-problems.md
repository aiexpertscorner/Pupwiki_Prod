## About Neapolitan Mastiffs
Unless you’ve been to Jurassic Park, you’ve never seen anything like a Mastino. These majestic guardians of startling appearance are massive, powerful dogs. The U.S. Neapolitan Mastiff Club describes their dog’s head as “astounding”—and give them credit for hitting upon just the right adjective. The profuse hanging wrinkles and folds, and pendulous lips, make a Mastino look like a marzipan Mastiff that’s been out in the sun too long. And yet, the breed’s inner dignity and nobility can only be described as beautiful. The Neapolitan Mastiff is a huge, powerful guarder whose astounding appearance has intimidated intruders since the days of ancient Rome. Wary with strangers, the profusely wrinkled Mastino is sweet, placid, and steady among loved ones.

### What to know about Neapolitan Mastiff health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Neapolitan Mastiff in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Neapolitan Mastiff are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Neapolitan Mastiff life expectancy of 7–9 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Neapolitan Mastiff health
**What are the most common health problems in Neapolitan Mastiffs?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Neapolitan Mastiff. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Neapolitan Mastiffs live?**
Neapolitan Mastiffs have a typical life expectancy of 7–9 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Neapolitan Mastiff?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Neapolitan Mastiff owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Reserved with strangers — early, thorough socialization is critical for this breed.
