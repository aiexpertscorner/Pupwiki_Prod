## How to approach Doxie-poo care
Prioritise formulas with an AAFCO adequacy statement — this confirms the recipe is complete and balanced. Named proteins should appear before grains in the ingredient list. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Doxie-poos are documented for joint conditions (ivdd, hip dysplasia) — look for formulas listing glucosamine and chondroitin as added ingredients, or plan to add a separate joint supplement after age 5. For a typical adult Doxie-poo (11.02–30.86 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 380 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 479–585 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Doxie-poo
Medium breeds like the Doxie-poo do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Doxie-poo needs fewer calories than an athletic one. Doxie-poos have a documented risk of joint conditions. Formulas with added glucosamine and chondroitin offer a convenient way to support joint health alongside the diet.
### Frequently asked questions about Doxie-poo feeding
**How much should I feed my Doxie-poo?**
A rough starting point for Doxie-poos (11.02–30.86 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Doxie-poo?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Doxie-poo from puppy to adult food?**
Most Doxie-poos can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
