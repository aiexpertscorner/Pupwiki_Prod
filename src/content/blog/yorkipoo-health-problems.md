## About Yorkipoos
Yorkipoo is a designer cross between the Yorkshire Terrier and the Poodle (Toy). This mix usually weighs about 7-15 pounds, often has a wavy or fleece-like coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Legg-Calve-Perthes disease, Hypoglycemia. Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Yorkipoo health planning
Yorkipoos have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Legg-Calve-Perthes disease, Hypoglycemia**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Yorkipoo often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Yorkipoo life expectancy of 10–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

**Should I get pet insurance for a Yorkipoo?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Yorkipoos. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.
### Care tips for Yorkipoo owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes of daily walks, play, and light training games.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
