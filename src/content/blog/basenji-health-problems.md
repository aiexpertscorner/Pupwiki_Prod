### What to know about Basenji health planning
Basenjis have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **kidney, eye problems, anaemia**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Basenji often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Basenji life expectancy of 13–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
