## About Miniature American Shepherds
Miniature American Shepherds are medium-sized (20–40 lbs), with active energy, heavy shedding, and a long coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

**How long do Miniature American Shepherds live?**

Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Miniature American Shepherd?**

### Care tips for Miniature American Shepherd owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes active exercise daily; thrives with a job or sport.
- Coat requires daily brushing to prevent matting; professional grooming every 6-8 weeks.
- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
