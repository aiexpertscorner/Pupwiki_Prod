## About Belgian Sheepdogs
Belgian Sheepdogs are large-sized (45–75 lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a long coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### What to know about Belgian Sheepdog health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Belgian Sheepdog in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Belgian Sheepdog are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Belgian Sheepdog life expectancy of 12–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Belgian Sheepdog owners
