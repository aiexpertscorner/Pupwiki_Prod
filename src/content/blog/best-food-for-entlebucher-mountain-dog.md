## How to choose for a Entlebucher Mountain Dog
Start with AAFCO compliance: the label should state the food is complete and balanced for the appropriate life stage. A clearly named protein (not just "meat" or "poultry") as the first ingredient is the next filter. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Entlebucher Mountain Dog is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. For a typical adult Entlebucher Mountain Dog (40–65 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 760 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 1094–1338 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.

### Frequently asked questions about Entlebucher Mountain Dog feeding
**How much should I feed my Entlebucher Mountain Dog?**
A rough starting point for Entlebucher Mountain Dogs (40–65 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Entlebucher Mountain Dog?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Entlebucher Mountain Dog from puppy to adult food?**
Most Entlebucher Mountain Dogs can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
