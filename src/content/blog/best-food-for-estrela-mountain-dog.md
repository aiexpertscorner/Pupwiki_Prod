## How to choose for a Estrela Mountain Dog
Start with AAFCO compliance: the label should state the food is complete and balanced for the appropriate life stage. A clearly named protein (not just "meat" or "poultry") as the first ingredient is the next filter. Large breeds benefit from formulas with controlled calcium-to-phosphorus ratios and moderate fat content to support joint health and avoid rapid growth issues. Estrela Mountain Dog has a lower activity level — choose a formula with controlled fat content to avoid obesity, which is a common risk for lower-energy dogs. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient. For a typical adult Estrela Mountain Dog (77–132 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 1269 kcal/day — with a calm-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 1371–1675 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.

### Frequently asked questions about Estrela Mountain Dog feeding
**How much should I feed my Estrela Mountain Dog?**
A rough starting point for Estrela Mountain Dogs (77–132 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Estrela Mountain Dog?**
Look for formulas specifically labelled for large breeds, which manage calcium and phosphorus ratios to support joint development. Avoid puppy formulas designed for small dogs even if your Estrela Mountain Dog is young.
**When should I switch my Estrela Mountain Dog from puppy to adult food?**
Large breeds should stay on a large-breed puppy formula until 18–24 months, then transition to an adult formula. Switching too early can disrupt joint development during the growth phase.
