## Why this guide exists for Sprollies
Sprollies are medium-sized (varies lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a silky coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
This page helps Sprollie people compare useful brands, products and services for a real care decision. It is also useful if you are still deciding whether a Sprollie fits your home, budget and routine.
