## How to choose for a Norwegian Elkhound
Start with AAFCO compliance: the label should state the food is complete and balanced for the appropriate life stage. A clearly named protein (not just "meat" or "poultry") as the first ingredient is the next filter. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient. For a typical adult Norwegian Elkhound (48–55 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 749 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 944–1154 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Norwegian Elkhound
Medium breeds like the Norwegian Elkhound do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Norwegian Elkhound needs fewer calories than an athletic one. Norwegian Elkhounds shed seasonally — look for formulas that include omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA from salmon or fish oil) to support coat health from the inside out.
### Frequently asked questions about Norwegian Elkhound feeding
**How much should I feed my Norwegian Elkhound?**
A rough starting point for Norwegian Elkhounds (48–55 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Norwegian Elkhound?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Norwegian Elkhound from puppy to adult food?**
Most Norwegian Elkhounds can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
