### What to know about Bouvier des Flandres health planning
Bouvier des Flandress have 1 documented genetic health condition: **hip problems**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Bouvier des Flandres are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Bouvier des Flandres life expectancy of 10–12 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
