## About Cavadoodles
Cavadoodle is a designer cross between the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Poodle (Miniature). This mix usually weighs about 26-40 pounds, often has a wavy or fleece-like coat, and often blends a calmer companion temperament with moderate activity needs from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Heart disease, PRA. Originally from United Kingdom.

### Care tips for Cavadoodle owners
- Does best with shorter daily walks, enrichment toys, and careful weight management.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
