Maltese are affectionate toy dogs weighing less than seven pounds, covered by a long, straight, silky coat. Beneath the all-white mantle is a compact body moving with a smooth and effortless gait. Maltese are low-shedding, long-lived, and happy to make new friends of all ages. Sometimes stubborn and willful, they respond well to rewards-based training. Toy and small breeds like the Maltese often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. ### Care tips for Maltese owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Low shedding — regular brushing prevents tangles; minimal shedding management.
- Coat requires daily brushing to prevent matting; professional grooming every 6-8 weeks.
- Very sociable and people-oriented — early socialization keeps friendliness well-directed.
