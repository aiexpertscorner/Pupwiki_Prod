## About Curly-Coated Retrievers
Curly-Coated Retrievers are large-sized (60–95 lbs), with active energy, low shedding, and a curly coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### What to know about Curly-Coated Retriever health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Curly-Coated Retriever in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Curly-Coated Retriever are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Curly-Coated Retriever life expectancy of 10–12 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
