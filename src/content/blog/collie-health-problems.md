## About Collies
Collies are large-sized (50–75 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Collies
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Collie (typical life expectancy: 12–14 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.
## How to approach Collie health
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to know about Collie health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Collie in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Collie are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Collie life expectancy of 12–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Collie health
**What are the most common health problems in Collies?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Collie. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Collies live?**
Collies have a typical life expectancy of 12–14 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Collie?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Collie owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.
