## About Cheagles
Cheagle is a designer cross between the Chihuahua and the Beagle. This mix usually weighs about 9-22 pounds, often lands somewhere between its parent coat types, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Heart disease, Obesity, Thyroid issues. Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Cheagle health planning
Cheagles have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **Heart disease, Obesity, Thyroid issues**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Cheagle often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Cheagle life expectancy of 12–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Cheagle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Seasonal coat changes can be intense—step up brushing during spring and fall shedding periods.
- Consistency matters—keep sessions short, reward-based, and started early for best results.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
