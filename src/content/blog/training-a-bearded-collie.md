### What to look for in training gear for a Bearded Collie
Bearded Collies respond best to consistent, reward-focused training. Keep sessions under 15 minutes, end on a success, and involve every person in the household — selective compliance is common when rules differ by person. For walking gear, start with a flat collar for ID and a front-clip harness for leash training — avoid using aversive tools (prong collars, choke chains) while building leash skills. A 6-foot leash for street work and a 20–30 foot long-line for recall practice cover 90% of training scenarios. Socialisation matters as much as obedience: expose your Bearded Collie to different surfaces, sounds, dogs, and strangers between 8 and 16 weeks. A well-socialised Bearded Collie with moderate obedience is more manageable than a precisely trained but under-socialised one. Many trainers recommend structured puppy classes over solo home training for this reason — the peer exposure is as valuable as the instruction.
### Frequently asked questions about Bearded Collie training
**How easy is it to train a Bearded Collie?**
Bearded Collies are moderately easy to train. They benefit from positive reinforcement methods and early socialisation. Most owners with some prior dog experience find them straightforward; first-time owners benefit from a puppy class.
**What training method works best for a Bearded Collie?**
Reward-based training (treats, praise, toy rewards) is the most effective and well-researched approach for Bearded Collies. Clicker training works well if you're precise with timing. Avoid aversive methods — they increase anxiety in dogs and can create secondary behavioural problems.
**At what age should I start training my Bearded Collie?**
Start training your Bearded Collie puppy from the day they arrive home — typically 8 weeks. Early socialisation (weeks 8–16) is the most sensitive developmental window. Enrol in a puppy class within the first two weeks for structured socialisation alongside basic obedience foundations.
