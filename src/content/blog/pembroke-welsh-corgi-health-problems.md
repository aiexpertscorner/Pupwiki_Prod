## About Pembroke Welsh Corgis
Pembroke Welsh Corgis are medium-sized (28–30 lbs), with active energy, heavy shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### Care tips for Pembroke Welsh Corgi owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes active exercise daily; thrives with a job or sport.
- Heavy shedder — brush 3-4× per week and deShed weekly to manage coat.
- Very sociable and people-oriented — early socialization keeps friendliness well-directed.
