## About Rottweilers
Rottweilers are large-sized (80–135 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a short coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Rottweilers
Rottweilers have 3 documented genetic health conditions: heart, elbow, hip problems. Typical life expectancy is 9–10 years.

### What to know about Rottweiler health planning
Rottweilers have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **heart, elbow, hip problems**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Rottweiler are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Rottweiler life expectancy of 9–10 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Rottweiler owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
