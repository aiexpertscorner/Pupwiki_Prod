## About Cavapoos
Cavapoo is a designer cross between the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Poodle (Miniature). This mix usually weighs about 11-24 pounds, often has a wavy or fleece-like coat, and often blends a calmer companion temperament with moderate activity needs from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Mitral Valve Disease (Heart), Syringomyelia. Originally from United Kingdom.

## How to approach a Cavapoo puppy's first year
Choose puppy-specific formulas, sizes and training tools. Avoid adult-strength supplements and training methods designed for mature dogs.
### What to prioritise in the first weeks with a Cavapoo puppy
Small-breed puppies like the Cavapoo have fast metabolisms and small stomachs — feed 3–4 small meals per day until 6 months, then twice daily. Use a small-breed puppy formula; avoid large-breed or all-breed blends with inappropriate calorie density. Schedule a vet visit within the first 72 hours of bringing your Cavapoo puppy home. The first-year vet schedule typically includes: 8-week vaccines, 12-week booster, 16-week final puppy booster, 6-month neuter/spay consultation, and 12-month first adult check. The critical socialisation window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds, surfaces, people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A puppy class started at 8–10 weeks provides peer exposure that home training alone cannot replicate.
### Frequently asked questions about Cavapoo puppy care
**What does a Cavapoo puppy need in the first week?**
In the first week: vet check within 72 hours, a small-breed puppy formula (3–4 small meals/day), a crate the right size for sleeping, and gentle exposure to household sounds. Avoid dog parks and public areas until vaccinations are complete.
**How do I socialise a Cavapoo puppy?**
The critical window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds (traffic, appliances, children), new surfaces (gravel, carpet, grass, pavement), new people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A structured puppy class started at 8–10 weeks is the most efficient route — it covers socialisation and basic obedience in one place. After 16 weeks the window closes — missed socialisation is harder (not impossible) to compensate for later.
**How much does a Cavapoo puppy cost in the first year?**
Beyond the purchase price (~$1,290 for a Cavapoo), the first year typically includes: food (~$263–355 for puppy-stage feeding), puppy vaccines and vet checks ($300–$600), supplies/setup ($200–$500), and spay/neuter ($200–$600). Total first-year cost is often 50–100% higher than subsequent years.
### Care tips for Cavapoo owners
- Does best with shorter daily walks, enrichment toys, and careful weight management.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
