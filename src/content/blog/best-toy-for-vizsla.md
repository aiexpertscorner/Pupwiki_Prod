## Frequently Asked Questions
### What toys are best for Vizslas?
Vizslas thrive with high-energy toys like fetch balls, tug ropes, and puzzle feeders. The most important factor is matching toy durability to your dog's chewing intensity — heavy-duty rubber like KONG Extreme is recommended for this breed.
### How often should I give my Vizsla new toys?
Rotate toys every 3–5 days to maintain novelty and engagement. Vizslas are smart dogs who quickly lose interest in familiar toys — rotation is especially important for this breed. Keep 4–6 toys active and rotate them.
### Are squeaky toys safe for Vizslas?
Squeaky toys are generally safe under supervision, but not for unsupervised chewing — the squeaker is a swallowing hazard once the toy is open. For this high-energy breed, plush squeaky toys are best used as interactive play toys, not chew toys.
