## About Saint Bernards
Saint Bernards are large-sized (120–180 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Saint Bernards
Saint Bernards have 3 documented genetic health conditions: heart, hip disorders, fatal stomach bloat. Typical life expectancy is 8–10 years.

### What to know about Saint Bernard health planning
Saint Bernards have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **heart, hip disorders, fatal stomach bloat**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Saint Bernard are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Saint Bernard life expectancy of 8–10 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
