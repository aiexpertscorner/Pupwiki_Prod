## How to choose for a Teddy Roosevelt Terrier
Look for AAFCO compliance — the label should confirm the formula is complete and balanced for your dog's life stage. A named protein (chicken, salmon, beef) should be the first ingredient listed. Small and toy breeds have faster metabolisms and do better with smaller kibble sizes and calorie-dense formulas — avoid large-breed formulations designed for slower growth. Teddy Roosevelt Terrier is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient. For a typical adult Teddy Roosevelt Terrier (8–25 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 324 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 466–570 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Teddy Roosevelt Terrier
Small and toy breeds like the Teddy Roosevelt Terrier have fast metabolisms — look for formulas with calorie-dense kibble sized for smaller jaws, and avoid large-breed blends designed for slower growth rates. Teddy Roosevelt Terriers are active dogs — a higher-protein formula (26–30% crude protein) supports muscle maintenance and sustained energy through their exercise demands. Teddy Roosevelt Terriers shed seasonally — look for formulas that include omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA from salmon or fish oil) to support coat health from the inside out.
### Frequently asked questions about Teddy Roosevelt Terrier feeding
**How much should I feed my Teddy Roosevelt Terrier?**
A rough starting point for Teddy Roosevelt Terriers (8–25 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Teddy Roosevelt Terrier?**
Small-breed formulas with higher protein and smaller kibble sizes are the right starting point. Avoid large-breed or generic "all sizes" formulas, which may have inappropriate calcium levels for fast metabolisms.
**When should I switch my Teddy Roosevelt Terrier from puppy to adult food?**
Small breeds typically transition to adult food around 12 months. Moving too late keeps them on higher-calorie puppy formulas that can cause weight gain in lower-activity adults.
