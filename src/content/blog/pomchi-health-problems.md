## About Pomchis
Pomchi is a designer cross between the Pomeranian and the Chihuahua. This mix usually weighs about 4-11 pounds, can inherit a dense double coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Hydrocephalus, Collapsing trachea.

### What to know about Pomchi health planning
Pomchis have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Hydrocephalus, Collapsing trachea**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Pomchi often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Pomchi life expectancy of 13–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Pomchi health
**What are the most common health problems in Pomchis?**
Pomchis have 2 documented genetic health conditions: Hydrocephalus, Collapsing trachea. These are breed-level statistical risks — not every Pomchi will develop these conditions. Your vet can recommend targeted screening based on your dog's individual history.
**How long do Pomchis live?**
Pomchis have a typical life expectancy of 13–16 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Pomchi?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Pomchis. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.
### Care tips for Pomchi owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes of daily walks, play, and light training games.
- Seasonal coat changes can be intense—step up brushing during spring and fall shedding periods.
- Consistency matters—keep sessions short, reward-based, and started early for best results.
- Watch heat and exercise tolerance closely, especially in warm weather or high humidity.
