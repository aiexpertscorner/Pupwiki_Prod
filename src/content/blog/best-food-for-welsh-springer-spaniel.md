## How to choose for a Welsh Springer Spaniel
Check for an AAFCO adequacy statement, which confirms the food meets minimum nutritional standards for the correct life stage. A named protein source (chicken, turkey, beef, salmon) should appear as the first ingredient. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Welsh Springer Spaniel is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain.
### What to look for in food for a Welsh Springer Spaniel
Medium breeds like the Welsh Springer Spaniel do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Welsh Springer Spaniels are active dogs — a higher-protein formula (26–30% crude protein) supports muscle maintenance and sustained energy through their exercise demands.
### Frequently asked questions about Welsh Springer Spaniel feeding
**How much should I feed my Welsh Springer Spaniel?**
Follow the feeding guide on your chosen formula and adjust based on body condition — you should be able to feel (but not see) the ribs. Most Welsh Springer Spaniels need 2–3 meals per day as adults.
**What food formula works best for a Welsh Springer Spaniel?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Welsh Springer Spaniel from puppy to adult food?**
Most Welsh Springer Spaniels can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
