## About Belgian Malinoiss
Belgian Malinoiss are large-sized (40–80 lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a short coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### What to know about Belgian Malinois health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Belgian Malinois in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Belgian Malinois are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Belgian Malinois life expectancy of 14–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

**How long do Belgian Malinoiss live?**
Belgian Malinoiss have a typical life expectancy of 14–16 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Belgian Malinois?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
