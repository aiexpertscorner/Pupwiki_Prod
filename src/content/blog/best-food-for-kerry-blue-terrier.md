## How to approach Kerry Blue Terrier care
Start with AAFCO compliance: the label should state the food is complete and balanced for the appropriate life stage. A clearly named protein (not just "meat" or "poultry") as the first ingredient is the next filter. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Kerry Blue Terriers have an elevated risk for cardiac conditions. Current evidence suggests caution with grain-free diets as a primary choice — consult your vet before selecting a formula, and ensure it meets AAFCO nutritional standards. For a typical adult Kerry Blue Terrier (33–40 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 580 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 731–893 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Kerry Blue Terrier
Medium breeds like the Kerry Blue Terrier do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Kerry Blue Terrier needs fewer calories than an athletic one.
### Frequently asked questions about Kerry Blue Terrier feeding
**How much should I feed my Kerry Blue Terrier?**
A rough starting point for Kerry Blue Terriers (33–40 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Kerry Blue Terrier?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Kerry Blue Terrier from puppy to adult food?**
Most Kerry Blue Terriers can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
