## Why Brittanys Need a Breed-Specific Toy
Brittanys are high-energy dogs that need mentally and physically engaging toys to prevent boredom and destructive behavior. As a highly trainable breed, they excel with puzzle toys and training games.
**Key Brittany characteristics affecting toy choice:**
- **Size:** Medium breed (30–40 lbs)
- **Energy level:** Needs Lots of Activity
- **Shedding:** Seasonal
- **Trainability:** Eager to Please
- **Lifespan:** 12–14 years

## Brittany-Specific Toy Tips
- Requires 90+ minutes vigorous exercise daily — fetch, agility, or long hikes.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What toys are best for Brittanys?
Brittanys thrive with high-energy toys like fetch balls, tug ropes, and puzzle feeders. The most important factor is matching toy durability to your dog's chewing intensity — heavy-duty rubber like KONG Extreme is recommended for this breed.
### How often should I give my Brittany new toys?
Rotate toys every 3–5 days to maintain novelty and engagement. Brittanys are smart dogs who quickly lose interest in familiar toys — rotation is especially important for this breed. Keep 4–6 toys active and rotate them.
### Are squeaky toys safe for Brittanys?
Squeaky toys are generally safe under supervision, but not for unsupervised chewing — the squeaker is a swallowing hazard once the toy is open. For this high-energy breed, plush squeaky toys are best used as interactive play toys, not chew toys.
