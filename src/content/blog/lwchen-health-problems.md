## About Löwchens
Löwchens are small-sized (10–15 lbs), with regular energy, minimal shedding, and a long coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.

### What to know about Löwchen health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Löwchen in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Löwchen often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Löwchen life expectancy of 15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

**How long do Löwchens live?**

**Should I get pet insurance for a Löwchen?**
