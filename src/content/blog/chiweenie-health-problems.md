## About Chiweenies
Chiweenie is a designer cross between the Chihuahua and the Dachshund. This mix usually weighs about 7-11 pounds, often lands somewhere between its parent coat types, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Intervertebral Disc Disease (IVDD), Dental crowding. Originally from Germany.

### What to know about Chiweenie health planning
Chiweenies have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Intervertebral Disc Disease (IVDD), Dental crowding**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Chiweenie often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Chiweenie life expectancy of 13–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Chiweenie owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes of daily walks, play, and light training games.
- Keep coat maintenance steady with weekly brushing and routine skin, ear, and nail checks.
- Consistency matters—keep sessions short, reward-based, and started early for best results.
- Protect the back with lean body condition, controlled jumping, and good traction at home.
