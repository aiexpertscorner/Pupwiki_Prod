### What to know about German Shepherd Dog health planning
German Shepherd Dogs have 8 documented genetic health conditions: **nerves, pancreas, blood (hemophilia), hip joints**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the German Shepherd Dog are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical German Shepherd Dog life expectancy of 7–10 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for German Shepherd Dog owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Heavy shedder — brush 3-4× per week and deShed weekly to manage coat.
- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
