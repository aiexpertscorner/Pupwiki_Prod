## About Norfolk Terriers
Norfolk Terriers are small-sized (11–12 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a wiry coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Norfolk Terriers
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Norfolk Terrier (typical life expectancy: 12–16 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.
## How to approach Norfolk Terrier health

### What to know about Norfolk Terrier health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Norfolk Terrier in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Norfolk Terrier often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Norfolk Terrier life expectancy of 12–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Norfolk Terrier health
**What are the most common health problems in Norfolk Terriers?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Norfolk Terrier. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Norfolk Terriers live?**
Norfolk Terriers have a typical life expectancy of 12–16 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Norfolk Terrier?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Norfolk Terrier owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
