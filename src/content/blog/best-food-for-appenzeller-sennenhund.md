### Frequently asked questions about Appenzeller Sennenhund feeding
**How much should I feed my Appenzeller Sennenhund?**
A rough starting point for Appenzeller Sennenhunds (48–70 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Appenzeller Sennenhund?**
Look for formulas specifically labelled for large breeds, which manage calcium and phosphorus ratios to support joint development. Avoid puppy formulas designed for small dogs even if your Appenzeller Sennenhund is young.
**When should I switch my Appenzeller Sennenhund from puppy to adult food?**
Large breeds should stay on a large-breed puppy formula until 18–24 months, then transition to an adult formula. Switching too early can disrupt joint development during the growth phase.
