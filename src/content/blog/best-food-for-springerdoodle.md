## How to approach Springerdoodle care
Look for AAFCO compliance — the label should confirm the formula is complete and balanced for your dog's life stage. A named protein (chicken, salmon, beef) should be the first ingredient listed. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. For a typical adult Springerdoodle (33.07–55.12 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 661 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 833–1018 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Springerdoodle
Medium breeds like the Springerdoodle do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Springerdoodle needs fewer calories than an athletic one.
### Frequently asked questions about Springerdoodle feeding
**How much should I feed my Springerdoodle?**
A rough starting point for Springerdoodles (33.07–55.12 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Springerdoodle?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Springerdoodle from puppy to adult food?**
Most Springerdoodles can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
