## Why this guide exists for Pitskys
Pitskys are medium-sized (varies lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a double coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
This page helps Pitsky people compare useful brands, products and services for a real care decision. It is also useful if you are still deciding whether a Pitsky fits your home, budget and routine.
