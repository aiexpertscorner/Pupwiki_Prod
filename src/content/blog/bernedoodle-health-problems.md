## About Bernedoodles
Bernedoodle is a designer cross between the Bernese Mountain Dog and the Poodle (Standard). This mix usually weighs about 55-99 pounds, often has a wavy or fleece-like coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Hip dysplasia, Elbow issues, Certain cancers. Originally from Switzerland.

### What to know about Bernedoodle health planning
Bernedoodles have 3 documented genetic health conditions: **Hip dysplasia, Elbow issues, Certain cancers**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Bernedoodle are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Bernedoodle life expectancy of 8–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Bernedoodle owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Keep body weight lean and monitor mobility to reduce stress on developing joints.
