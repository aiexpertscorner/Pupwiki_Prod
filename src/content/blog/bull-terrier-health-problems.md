## About Bull Terriers
Bull Terriers are large-sized (50–70 lbs), with active energy, seasonal shedding, and a short coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Bull Terriers
Bull Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: heart problems, zinc metabolism disorder. Typical life expectancy is 12–13 years.
- **Heart problems**: Ask your vet about recommended screening and prevention for this condition in Bull Terriers.
- **Zinc metabolism disorder**: Ask your vet about recommended screening and prevention for this condition in Bull Terriers.

## How to approach Bull Terrier health
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to know about Bull Terrier health planning
Bull Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **heart problems, zinc metabolism disorder**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Large breeds like the Bull Terrier are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Bull Terrier life expectancy of 12–13 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Bull Terrier health
**What are the most common health problems in Bull Terriers?**
Bull Terriers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: heart problems, zinc metabolism disorder. These are breed-level statistical risks — not every Bull Terrier will develop these conditions. Your vet can recommend targeted screening based on your dog's individual history.
**How long do Bull Terriers live?**
Bull Terriers have a typical life expectancy of 12–13 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Bull Terrier?**
With 2 documented genetic conditions, pet insurance can help manage financial risk for Bull Terriers. Get a quote before your puppy's first vet visit — some conditions can be flagged as pre-existing quickly.
### Care tips for Bull Terrier owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes active exercise daily; thrives with a job or sport.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
