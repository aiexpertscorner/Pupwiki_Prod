## How to choose for a English Cocker Spaniel
Measure your dog stretched out fully, add 12 inches, then match to the bed's stated usable sleep surface. Check whether the cover is machine washable.
### What to look for in a bed for a English Cocker Spaniel
Mid-size breeds like the English Cocker Spaniel do well in rectangular flat beds (30–36 inch) with a removable washable cover. Washability matters more than most buyers expect — plan to launder every 2–3 weeks. Orthopedic foam matters most once your English Cocker Spaniel reaches 7+ years — earlier than most owners expect for medium breeds. If your dog is already showing joint stiffness, prioritise pressure-relief foam over aesthetics. Choose a cover with a zipper rather than a slip-on sleeve so the insert stays in place even when your English Cocker Spaniel rearranges it overnight.
### Frequently asked questions about English Cocker Spaniel beds
**What size bed does a English Cocker Spaniel need?**
Measure your English Cocker Spaniel stretched out and add 12 inches. Medium breeds (30–40 lb) typically need a 30–36 inch bed. If they hang off the edges regularly, size up.
**Is an orthopedic bed worth it for a English Cocker Spaniel?**
For any dog over 7 years, or one already showing joint stiffness, an orthopedic foam bed offers meaningful pressure relief. For younger healthy English Cocker Spaniels, a quality foam bed with a washable cover is adequate — you can upgrade as they age.
**How often should I replace my English Cocker Spaniel's bed?**
Most dog beds need replacing every 1–2 years under regular use. Signs it's time: visible compression of the foam (it no longer springs back), persistent odour after washing, or your English Cocker Spaniel choosing the floor over the bed.
