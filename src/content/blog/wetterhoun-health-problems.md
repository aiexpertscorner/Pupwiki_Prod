## About Wetterhouns
The Wetterhoun, although originally bred for hunting otters, now make excellent companions, watchdogs, and can excel in a variety of sports due to their perseverant nature. The Wetterhoun is almost never seen apart from its owner; they enjoy their company as much as the owner enjoys theirs.

### What to know about Wetterhoun health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Wetterhoun in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Wetterhoun are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Wetterhoun life expectancy of 13 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
