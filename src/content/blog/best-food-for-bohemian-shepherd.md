## How to choose for a Bohemian Shepherd
An AAFCO-approved formula with a whole protein source — chicken, beef, salmon, or turkey — listed first is the baseline standard. Avoid formulas where the first ingredient is a grain or generic "meat by-products". Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. For a typical adult Bohemian Shepherd (37–60 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 717 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 904–1104 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Bohemian Shepherd
Medium breeds like the Bohemian Shepherd do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Bohemian Shepherd needs fewer calories than an athletic one.
### Frequently asked questions about Bohemian Shepherd feeding
**How much should I feed my Bohemian Shepherd?**
A rough starting point for Bohemian Shepherds (37–60 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Bohemian Shepherd?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Bohemian Shepherd from puppy to adult food?**
Most Bohemian Shepherds can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
