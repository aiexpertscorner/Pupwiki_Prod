## About English Springer Spaniels

The double coat comes in several colors and patterns, the ears are long and lush, and the kindly, trusting expression of the eyes is a cherished hallmark of the breed. ## How to approach a English Springer Spaniel puppy's first year
Choose puppy-specific formulas, sizes and training tools. Avoid adult-strength supplements and training methods designed for mature dogs.
### What to prioritise in the first weeks with a English Springer Spaniel puppy
Medium-breed puppies do well on standard puppy formulas — look for AAFCO "growth" or "all life stages" labelling. Transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Schedule a vet visit within the first 72 hours of bringing your English Springer Spaniel puppy home. The first-year vet schedule typically includes: 8-week vaccines, 12-week booster, 16-week final puppy booster, 6-month neuter/spay consultation, and 12-month first adult check. The critical socialisation window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds, surfaces, people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A puppy class started at 8–10 weeks provides peer exposure that home training alone cannot replicate.
### Frequently asked questions about English Springer Spaniel puppy care
**What does a English Springer Spaniel puppy need in the first week?**
In the first week: vet check within 72 hours, a puppy formula appropriate for medium breeds (3 meals/day until 6 months), crate introduction with positive reinforcement, and household exposure. Avoid public dog areas until vaccines are complete.
**How do I socialise a English Springer Spaniel puppy?**
The critical window is 8–16 weeks. Each week, introduce new sounds (traffic, appliances, children), new surfaces (gravel, carpet, grass, pavement), new people, and vaccinated calm dogs. A structured puppy class started at 8–10 weeks is the most efficient route — it covers socialisation and basic obedience in one place. After 16 weeks the window closes — missed socialisation is harder (not impossible) to compensate for later.
**How much does a English Springer Spaniel puppy cost in the first year?**
Beyond the purchase price (~$615 for a English Springer Spaniel), the first year typically includes: food (~$396–536 for puppy-stage feeding), puppy vaccines and vet checks ($300–$600), supplies/setup ($200–$500), and spay/neuter ($200–$600). Total first-year cost is often 50–100% higher than subsequent years.
### Care tips for English Springer Spaniel owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Highly trainable — excels with positive reinforcement; start obedience classes early.
