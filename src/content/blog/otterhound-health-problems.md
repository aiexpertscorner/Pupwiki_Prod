## About Otterhounds
Otterhounds are large-sized (80–115 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a rough coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Otterhounds
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Otterhound (typical life expectancy: 10–13 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.

### What to know about Otterhound health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Otterhound in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Otterhound are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Otterhound life expectancy of 10–13 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Otterhound health
**What are the most common health problems in Otterhounds?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Otterhound. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Otterhounds live?**
Otterhounds have a typical life expectancy of 10–13 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Otterhound?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Otterhound owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
