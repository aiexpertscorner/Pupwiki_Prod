## How to choose for a Old English Sheepdog
An AAFCO-approved formula with a whole protein source — chicken, beef, salmon, or turkey — listed first is the baseline standard. Avoid formulas where the first ingredient is a grain or generic "meat by-products". Large breeds benefit from formulas with controlled calcium-to-phosphorus ratios and moderate fat content to support joint health and avoid rapid growth issues. Old English Sheepdogs are documented for joint conditions (hip problems) — look for formulas listing glucosamine and chondroitin as added ingredients, or plan to add a separate joint supplement after age 5. For a typical adult Old English Sheepdog (60–100 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 1035 kcal/day — with a regular-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 1304–1594 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Old English Sheepdog
Large breeds benefit from formulas with a controlled calcium-to-phosphorus ratio. Look for products specifically labelled for large or giant breeds, as these manage growth rate and joint load more carefully. Match calorie density to your individual dog's actual activity level, not just the breed average — a less active Old English Sheepdog needs fewer calories than an athletic one. Old English Sheepdogs have a documented risk of joint conditions. Formulas with added glucosamine and chondroitin offer a convenient way to support joint health alongside the diet.
### Frequently asked questions about Old English Sheepdog feeding
**How much should I feed my Old English Sheepdog?**
A rough starting point for Old English Sheepdogs (60–100 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Old English Sheepdog?**
Look for formulas specifically labelled for large breeds, which manage calcium and phosphorus ratios to support joint development. Avoid puppy formulas designed for small dogs even if your Old English Sheepdog is young.
**When should I switch my Old English Sheepdog from puppy to adult food?**
Large breeds should stay on a large-breed puppy formula until 18–24 months, then transition to an adult formula. Switching too early can disrupt joint development during the growth phase.
