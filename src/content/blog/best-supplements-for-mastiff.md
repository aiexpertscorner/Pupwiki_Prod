## How to approach supplements for Mastiffs

### What to look for in supplements for a Mastiff
⚠️ Always discuss supplements with your vet before starting. Supplements are not regulated as rigorously as medications — quality and dosing vary widely between products. Mastiffs have documented joint conditions (hip, heart problems). Glucosamine and chondroitin are among the most studied supplements for joint support in dogs — discuss appropriate timing with your vet before symptoms appear. When evaluating supplements, prioritise products with NASC (National Animal Supplement Council) certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for third-party testing, and a clearly stated active-ingredient concentration on the label.
### Frequently asked questions about Mastiff supplements
**What supplements are most relevant for a Mastiff?**
Mastiffs have documented joint conditions — glucosamine (500–1,000 mg/day depending on weight), chondroitin (400–800 mg/day), and omega-3 fatty acids are among the most studied options for joint support in dogs. Confirm dosing with your vet.
**Are supplements safe for Mastiffs?**
Dog supplements are not FDA-regulated the same way medications are — quality varies significantly between brands. Choose products with NASC certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party testing, and clearly stated active ingredient concentrations. Always disclose supplement use to your vet, as interactions with medications and health conditions are possible.
**At what age should I start supplements for a Mastiff?**
For large breeds like the Mastiff, joint supplements can be introduced as early as age 3–5 — before symptoms develop — if your vet agrees. This is particularly relevant for breeds with documented joint conditions. Don't wait until limping or stiffness appears if genetic risk is high.
