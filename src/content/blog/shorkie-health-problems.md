## About Shorkies
Shorkie is a designer cross between the Shih Tzu and the Yorkshire Terrier. This mix usually weighs about 7-15 pounds, often has a softer silky coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Brachycephalic issues, Dental disease. Originally from China / United Kingdom.

### What to know about Shorkie health planning
Shorkies have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Brachycephalic issues, Dental disease**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Shorkie often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Shorkie life expectancy of 10–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Shorkie owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes of daily walks, play, and light training games.
- Keep coat maintenance steady with weekly brushing and routine skin, ear, and nail checks.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Watch heat and exercise tolerance closely, especially in warm weather or high humidity.
