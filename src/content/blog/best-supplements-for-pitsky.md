## About Pitskys
Pitsky is a designer cross between the American Staffordshire Terrier and the Siberian Husky. This mix usually weighs about 33-77 pounds, can inherit a dense double coat, and often blends an active, people-oriented temperament from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Hip dysplasia, Allergies, Heart issues. Originally from United States / Russia.

## How to approach supplements for Pitskys
⚠️ Always consult your vet before adding supplements or making changes to your dog's health routine. This page is for comparison and planning only.
### What to look for in supplements for a Pitsky
⚠️ Always discuss supplements with your vet before starting. Supplements are not regulated as rigorously as medications — quality and dosing vary widely between products. Pitskys have documented joint conditions (hip dysplasia, allergies, heart issues). Glucosamine and chondroitin are among the most studied supplements for joint support in dogs — discuss appropriate timing with your vet before symptoms appear. When evaluating supplements, prioritise products with NASC (National Animal Supplement Council) certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for third-party testing, and a clearly stated active-ingredient concentration on the label.
### Frequently asked questions about Pitsky supplements
**What supplements are most relevant for a Pitsky?**
Pitskys have documented joint conditions — glucosamine (500–1,000 mg/day depending on weight), chondroitin (400–800 mg/day), and omega-3 fatty acids are among the most studied options for joint support in dogs. Confirm dosing with your vet.
**Are supplements safe for Pitskys?**
Dog supplements are not FDA-regulated the same way medications are — quality varies significantly between brands. Choose products with NASC certification, a published Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from third-party testing, and clearly stated active ingredient concentrations. Always disclose supplement use to your vet, as interactions with medications and health conditions are possible.
**At what age should I start supplements for a Pitsky?**
Most supplements are most relevant from middle age onward (5–7 years depending on size). Puppies should generally not receive adult-strength supplements — ask your vet about puppy-appropriate options if indicated.
### Care tips for Pitsky owners
- Needs 60-90 minutes of purposeful daily exercise plus mental work to stay balanced.
- Seasonal coat changes can be intense—step up brushing during spring and fall shedding periods.
- Consistency matters—keep sessions short, reward-based, and started early for best results.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
