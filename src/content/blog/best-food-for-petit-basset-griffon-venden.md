## How to choose for a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen
Check for an AAFCO adequacy statement, which confirms the food meets minimum nutritional standards for the correct life stage. A named protein source (chicken, turkey, beef, salmon) should appear as the first ingredient. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. For a typical adult Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen (25–40 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 533 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 768–938 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.

### Frequently asked questions about Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen feeding
**How much should I feed my Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen?**
A rough starting point for Petit Basset Griffon Vendéens (25–40 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Petit Basset Griffon Vendéen from puppy to adult food?**
Most Petit Basset Griffon Vendéens can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
