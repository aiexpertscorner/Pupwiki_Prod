## About Beagliers
Beaglier is a designer cross between the Beagle and the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel. This mix usually weighs about 11-24 pounds, often has a softer silky coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Syringomyelia, Ear infections. Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Beaglier health planning
Beagliers have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Syringomyelia, Ear infections**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Beaglier often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Beaglier life expectancy of 11–15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
