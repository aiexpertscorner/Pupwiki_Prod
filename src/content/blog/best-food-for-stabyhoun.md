## How to approach Stabyhoun care
Start with AAFCO compliance: the label should state the food is complete and balanced for the appropriate life stage. A clearly named protein (not just "meat" or "poultry") as the first ingredient is the next filter. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Stabyhoun is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. High-shedding and double-coated breeds often benefit from omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) — look for formulas with salmon or fish oil as a listed ingredient. For a typical adult Stabyhoun (40–60 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 727 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 1047–1279 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Stabyhoun
Medium breeds like the Stabyhoun do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Stabyhouns are active dogs — a higher-protein formula (26–30% crude protein) supports muscle maintenance and sustained energy through their exercise demands. Stabyhouns shed seasonally — look for formulas that include omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA from salmon or fish oil) to support coat health from the inside out.
### Frequently asked questions about Stabyhoun feeding
**How much should I feed my Stabyhoun?**
A rough starting point for Stabyhouns (40–60 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Stabyhoun?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Stabyhoun from puppy to adult food?**
Most Stabyhouns can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
