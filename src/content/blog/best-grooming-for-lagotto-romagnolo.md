## How to approach Lagotto Romagnolo care
Compare service area, booking lead time, insurance held by the provider, emergency protocols and genuine reviews before committing to a care service.
### What to look for in grooming tools for a Lagotto Romagnolo
Lagotto Romagnolos have a curly coat that mats if neglected — a wide-tooth comb used after each brush session catches tangles before they tighten. Professional trims every 6–8 weeks keep the coat manageable. Bathing frequency depends on lifestyle: every 4–6 weeks for a dog that mostly stays indoors, more often if they swim or dig regularly. Over-bathing strips protective oils — use a pH-balanced dog shampoo rather than human products. Nail trims are often the most neglected part of Lagotto Romagnolo grooming. Nails that click on hard floors are already too long — plan for trims every 3–4 weeks or use a grinder if your dog is sensitive to clippers.
### Frequently asked questions about Lagotto Romagnolo grooming
**How often should I groom a Lagotto Romagnolo?**
Lagotto Romagnolos have a curly coat that should be brushed at least 3–4 times per week to prevent matting. Professional grooming every 6–8 weeks helps maintain coat health and reduces the at-home workload.
**Do Lagotto Romagnolos shed a lot?**
Lagotto Romagnolos have minimal shedding. It's manageable with a consistent brushing routine — a quality slicker brush or rubber grooming glove catches most loose hair before it reaches your furniture.
**Can I groom a Lagotto Romagnolo at home?**
Home grooming is possible with the right tools — slicker brush, undercoat rake, and nail grinder — but many Lagotto Romagnolo owners supplement with professional grooming 3–4 times per year to maintain coat condition and avoid burn-out.
