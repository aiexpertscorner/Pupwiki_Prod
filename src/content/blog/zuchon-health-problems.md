## About Zuchons
Zuchon is a designer cross between the Shih Tzu and the Bichon Frise. This mix usually weighs about 11-18 pounds, often shows a mixed coat type, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Bladder stones, Clogged tear ducts. Originally from China.

### What to know about Zuchon health planning
Zuchons have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Bladder stones, Clogged tear ducts**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Zuchon often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Zuchon life expectancy of 12–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
