## About Affenpinschers
Affenpinschers are small-sized (7–10 lbs), with regular energy, seasonal shedding, and a rough coat. The recommendations below are matched to these traits.
### Common health concerns for Affenpinschers
No breed-specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Affenpinscher (typical life expectancy: 12–15 years). Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and weight management — applies to all breeds. Discuss appropriate screening schedules with your vet.

### What to know about Affenpinscher health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Affenpinscher in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Toy and small breeds like the Affenpinscher often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Affenpinscher life expectancy of 12–15 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
### Frequently asked questions about Affenpinscher health
**What are the most common health problems in Affenpinschers?**
No specific genetic ailments are currently documented for the Affenpinscher. Standard preventive care — annual vet checks, dental hygiene, parasite prevention, and healthy weight — applies to all dogs. Ask your vet whether any breed-specific screening is recommended.
**How long do Affenpinschers live?**
Affenpinschers have a typical life expectancy of 12–15 years. Individual lifespan depends on genetics, weight management, preventive care, and whether any genetic conditions develop. Annual vet visits and maintaining a healthy weight are among the most evidence-backed longevity factors for dogs.
**Should I get pet insurance for a Affenpinscher?**
Pet insurance is worth considering for any dog. Even without breed-specific genetic conditions, unexpected accidents and illnesses affect most dogs over a lifetime. Compare plans by waiting period, reimbursement model, and annual or per-condition limits.
### Care tips for Affenpinscher owners
- Benefits from 30-60 minutes daily exercise — walks, play sessions, or swimming.
- Seasonal shedder — daily brushing during spring and fall coat blows is essential.
- Brush 2-3× weekly to maintain coat condition and reduce shedding around the home.
- Very sociable and people-oriented — early socialization keeps friendliness well-directed.
