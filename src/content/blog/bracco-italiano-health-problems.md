## About Bracco Italianos
Tough and adapted to all types of hunting, the Bracco Italiano is reliable, intelligent, docile and easy to train. He has a powerful appearance, with lean limbs, well-developed muscles and a sculpted head. In English, he is the Italian Pointing Dog. The Bracco Italiano is one of the oldest of the pointing breeds. It was introduced into the United States in the 1990s and has remained true to its heritage as a versatile gun dog. It is gentle in the home and tireless in the field.

### What to know about Bracco Italiano health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the Bracco Italiano in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the Bracco Italiano are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Bracco Italiano life expectancy of 10–14 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
