## About Havashires
Havashire is a designer cross between the Havanese and the Yorkshire Terrier. This mix usually weighs about 7-13 pounds, often has a softer silky coat, and often blends a balanced mix of playfulness and settle-down time from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Liver shunts, Dental decay. Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Havashire health planning
Havashires have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Liver shunts, Dental decay**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Havashire often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Havashire life expectancy of 12–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
