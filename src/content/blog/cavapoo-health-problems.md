## About Cavapoos
Cavapoo is a designer cross between the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel and the Poodle (Miniature). This mix usually weighs about 11-24 pounds, often has a wavy or fleece-like coat, and often blends a calmer companion temperament with moderate activity needs from both sides of the pedigree. Common monitoring areas include Mitral Valve Disease (Heart), Syringomyelia. Originally from United Kingdom.

### What to know about Cavapoo health planning
Cavapoos have 2 documented genetic health conditions: **Mitral Valve Disease (Heart), Syringomyelia**. Knowing these in advance helps you choose the right insurance timing and ask targeted screening questions at vet appointments. Toy and small breeds like the Cavapoo often live longer but are more prone to dental disease, luxating patellas, and tracheal collapse. Annual dental checks from age 2 onward are more important for small breeds than most owners realise. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical Cavapoo life expectancy of 11–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.

### Care tips for Cavapoo owners
- Does best with shorter daily walks, enrichment toys, and careful weight management.
- Coat can mat quickly—brush several times per week and plan regular professional grooming.
- Usually responds well to positive-reinforcement training and early socialization.
- Routine cardiac checkups are worth prioritizing if murmurs or exercise intolerance appear.
