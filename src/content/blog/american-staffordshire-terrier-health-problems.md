## About American Staffordshire Terriers
AmStaffs are stocky, muscular bull-type terriers standing 17 to 19 inches at the shoulder. ### What to know about American Staffordshire Terrier health planning
No specific genetic ailments are documented for the American Staffordshire Terrier in current breed data. Standard preventive care still applies. Large breeds like the American Staffordshire Terrier are statistically more prone to joint issues and shorter lifespans. Vet visits every 6 months from age 6 onward are generally recommended over the standard annual check. For any breed with documented genetic conditions, consider enrolling in pet insurance before symptoms appear — most policies exclude pre-existing conditions. With a typical American Staffordshire Terrier life expectancy of 12–16 years, insurance decisions made in the first year can significantly affect lifetime vet coverage.
