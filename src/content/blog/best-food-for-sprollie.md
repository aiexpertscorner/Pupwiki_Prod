## How to approach Sprollie care
Check for an AAFCO adequacy statement, which confirms the food meets minimum nutritional standards for the correct life stage. A named protein source (chicken, turkey, beef, salmon) should appear as the first ingredient. Medium breeds do well on standard adult maintenance formulas — prioritise named protein sources and appropriate calorie density for their activity level. Sprollie is an active breed — a higher-protein, moderate-fat formula supports sustained energy without excess weight gain. Sprollies are documented for joint conditions (hip dysplasia, cea) — look for formulas listing glucosamine and chondroitin as added ingredients, or plan to add a separate joint supplement after age 5. For a typical adult Sprollie (33.07–48.5 lbs), the estimated resting energy requirement is around 627 kcal/day — with a active-energy activity factor, daily intake sits roughly in the 903–1103 kcal range. Most quality kibbles list kcal/cup on the bag; use this to portion accurately rather than relying on the default "suggested feeding" table alone.
### What to look for in food for a Sprollie
Medium breeds like the Sprollie do well on standard adult maintenance formulas. Prioritise a named protein source in the first ingredient and an AAFCO adequacy statement for the right life stage. Sprollies are active dogs — a higher-protein formula (26–30% crude protein) supports muscle maintenance and sustained energy through their exercise demands. Sprollies have a documented risk of joint conditions. Formulas with added glucosamine and chondroitin offer a convenient way to support joint health alongside the diet.
### Frequently asked questions about Sprollie feeding
**How much should I feed my Sprollie?**
A rough starting point for Sprollies (33.07–48.5 lbs) is 1–2 cups per day for smaller adults and 3–4 cups for larger ones — always follow the feeding guidelines on the specific formula and adjust based on body condition, not just weight.
**What food formula works best for a Sprollie?**
An AAFCO-compliant adult maintenance formula with a named protein source (not "meat meal") as the first ingredient is the right baseline. Fresh or freeze-dried toppers can add variety without the commitment of a full diet switch.
**When should I switch my Sprollie from puppy to adult food?**
Most Sprollies can transition to adult food between 12 and 15 months. Transition gradually over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
