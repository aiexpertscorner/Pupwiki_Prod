## Common Questions

**How often should I bathe my dog?**
Most dogs benefit from bathing every 4–6 weeks. Over-bathing strips natural skin oils. Dogs with skin conditions, allergies, or who spend time outdoors may need more or less frequent bathing — ask your vet for guidance specific to your dog.

**Does waterless shampoo actually clean dogs?**
Waterless shampoo reduces odour and surface dirt effectively. It does not replace water bathing for dogs who've rolled in something, have heavily soiled coats, or need medicated shampoo treatment. Think of it as "good enough" freshening rather than a full clean.

**My dog is terrified of baths. What should I do?**
For bath-phobic dogs, start desensitisation long before bath day. Bring the dog near the bath without bathing. Reward calm behaviour. Progress to running water at a distance, then touch, then brief bathing. This takes weeks — but makes every future bath easier.
