## Common Questions

**Is fresh dog food worth the price?**
Published research shows benefits for digestibility and stool quality. Whether the benefits justify 3–10x the cost of quality kibble is a personal and financial decision. Many owners find a hybrid approach (fresh topper on kibble) gives most of the palatability benefit at a lower cost.

**How do I transition to fresh food?**
Both services recommend a 7-day transition: start with 25% fresh / 75% current food, increase to 50/50 at day 3, 75/25 at day 5, then 100% fresh by day 7. Fast transitions cause digestive upset.

**Can large breed dogs eat fresh food?**
Yes — both services account for breed size in their portioning. Large breeds require more careful calorie management. Both Farmer's Dog and Ollie allow you to specify breed and target weight for custom portions.
