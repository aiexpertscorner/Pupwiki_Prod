## Common Questions

**How accurate are at-home dog DNA tests?**
Both Embark and Wisdom Panel claim >99% accuracy for breed identification. Third-party comparisons generally confirm this for purebred and recent-generation mixed breeds. Accuracy for highly mixed dogs (5+ breeds) varies more.

**Does Wisdom Panel Essential include health screening?**
Wisdom Panel Essential does not include health screening. The Wisdom Panel Premium upgrade ($159.99) adds 25+ health tests. For health screening, Embark's standard kit includes 210+ conditions.

**How long do results take?**
Embark typically delivers results in 2–4 weeks from when the lab receives your swab. Wisdom Panel Essential usually delivers in 2–3 weeks. Both ship the kit quickly; the wait is in processing.
