## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the best dog food for Chesapeake Bay Retrievers?
For Chesapeake Bay Retrievers, we recommend Purina Pro Plan Large Breed Adult. Large breed formulas with controlled calcium (1.0–1.8% DM) and glucosamine are essential for this breed. Always transition foods gradually over 7–10 days.
### How much should I feed my Chesapeake Bay Retriever?
A typical adult Chesapeake Bay Retriever weighing 55–80 lbs needs 3–4 cups of dry food per day, split into two meals. Use the bag's guidelines as a starting point and adjust based on body condition score. You should be able to feel but not see the ribs.
### Do Chesapeake Bay Retrievers have food allergies?
Chesapeake Bay Retrievers can develop food sensitivities, most commonly to proteins (chicken, beef) rather than grains. If you notice itching, ear infections, or digestive issues, discuss an elimination diet with your vet. Novel protein formulas (salmon, duck, venison) work well for sensitive Chesapeake Bay Retrievers.
### Is grain-free food good for Chesapeake Bay Retrievers?
Unless your Chesapeake Bay Retriever has a confirmed grain allergy, grain-free is not necessary and may carry additional cardiac risk (FDA DCM investigation). High-quality grain-inclusive formulas from established brands like Purina Pro Plan and Hill's are the vet-recommended choice.
