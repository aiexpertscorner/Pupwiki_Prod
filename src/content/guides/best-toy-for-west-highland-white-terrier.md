## Frequently Asked Questions
### What toys are best for West Highland White Terriers?
West Highland White Terriers enjoy moderate play with durable chew toys and interactive puzzles. The most important factor is matching toy durability to your dog's chewing intensity — standard rubber toys are usually sufficient.
### How often should I give my West Highland White Terrier new toys?
Rotate toys every 3–5 days to maintain novelty and engagement. West Highland White Terriers benefit from the mental stimulation of encountering "new" toys regularly. Keep 4–6 toys active and rotate them.
### Are squeaky toys safe for West Highland White Terriers?
Squeaky toys are generally safe under supervision, but not for unsupervised chewing — the squeaker is a swallowing hazard once the toy is open. Monitor your dog during play and remove damaged toys immediately.
