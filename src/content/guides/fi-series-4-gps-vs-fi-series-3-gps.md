## Common Questions

**Can I use my Series 2 subscription on a Series 3 collar?**
Yes — Fi subscriptions transfer between collar generations. You pay for the collar hardware upgrade but keep your existing plan.

**How much does the Fi subscription cost?**
Fi offers monthly ($14.99), annual ($8.25/month), and multi-year plans. The annual plan is the best value for long-term users.

**Does Fi work for small breeds?**
Fi Series 3 starts at XS collar size (8–12 inch neck). The module is relatively light and sits on top of the collar. Check the specific size range for your breed.
