## Frequently Asked Questions
### What toys are best for Bernese Mountain Dogs?
Bernese Mountain Dogs thrive with high-energy toys like fetch balls, tug ropes, and puzzle feeders. The most important factor is matching toy durability to your dog's chewing intensity — heavy-duty rubber like KONG Extreme is recommended for this breed.
### How often should I give my Bernese Mountain Dog new toys?
Rotate toys every 3–5 days to maintain novelty and engagement. Bernese Mountain Dogs benefit from the mental stimulation of encountering "new" toys regularly. Keep 4–6 toys active and rotate them.
### Are squeaky toys safe for Bernese Mountain Dogs?
Squeaky toys are generally safe under supervision, but not for unsupervised chewing — the squeaker is a swallowing hazard once the toy is open. For this high-energy breed, plush squeaky toys are best used as interactive play toys, not chew toys.
